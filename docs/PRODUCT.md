# Product contract

Spend answers which external services a project uses, why Spend believes that, who pays, how much it costs, and what changed. A repository scan creates explainable candidates; a human confirms or ignores them. Billing remains independent because detecting Vercel does not prove which Vercel account pays.

The first production is private YoDev software. Public signup, commercial subscriptions, automated provider billing, currency conversion, bank sync, notifications, AI discovery and write access to external services are deliberately outside V1.

The principal success signal after launch is repeated weekly use to review discoveries, failed scans and potential waste—not the number of providers in the catalog.
